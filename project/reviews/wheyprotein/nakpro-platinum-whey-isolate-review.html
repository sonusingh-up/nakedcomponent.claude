The verdict

Naked Compound · 8.5 / 10

The best-value verified WPI in India right now. Unflavoured is the better buy — not just on price, but on protein purity per gram.

Nakpro Platinum is a Bengaluru-made whey protein isolate from cross-flow microfiltrated imported whey, independently verified by Eurofins via Trustified in April 2023. The unflavoured version delivers 31g protein from a 34g scoop — 91.2% protein by weight, comfortably above the 90% threshold that defines a true isolate. No amino acid additions. No added sugar. No free BCAAs padding the nitrogen reading.

At roughly ₹1,700–2,099 per kilogram on Amazon.in, the cost per gram of protein runs ₹7.3–8.0 depending on variant. That is 3.7× cheaper than ON Gold Standard (₹27.5/g) and roughly in line with Nutrabay Gold WPI (₹9.6/g) — except Nakpro hits a higher protein percentage per 100g, and both carry active independent lab certifications.

The real limitation here is Nakpro's own product lineup. The brand sells at least six variations under the "Platinum" name — standard flavoured, unflavoured raw, Platinum Plus+ (enzymes + vitamins), Platinum ISO Zero, Platinum with Creatine, and Platinum Plus 2× serving. The protein content differs across these. The Trustified certification covers the core Platinum Whey Isolate. Buying a "Platinum" variant without checking which one you're getting is a genuine purchasing risk.

Whey protein isolate — mechanism and why form matters here

Cross-flow microfiltration is a cold-process membrane separation technique that removes fat and lactose from sweet whey while concentrating the protein fraction. Unlike ion-exchange chromatography (another WPI production method), cross-flow microfiltration preserves the native conformation of whey proteins — immunoglobulins, lactoferrin, beta-lactoglobulin, alpha-lactalbumin — rather than selectively purifying beta-lactoglobulin at the expense of bioactive fractions.1

For muscle protein synthesis, the mechanism runs through leucine. Whey protein is approximately 11% leucine by amino acid composition. Leucine activates the mTORC1 kinase complex in skeletal muscle — specifically, it works through the Ragulator-Rag GTPase pathway to translocate mTORC1 to the lysosomal membrane where it gets activated by Rheb, triggering downstream phosphorylation of p70S6K and 4E-BP1, both of which accelerate ribosomal translation of muscle-specific mRNAs. The threshold for maximal mTORC1 activation in trained adults is approximately 2–3g leucine per dose.2 A 34g scoop of Nakpro Platinum unflavoured delivers approximately 3.1–3.3g leucine — above this threshold without requiring two scoops. See: L-Leucine entry →

The practical difference between a true WPI (greater than 90% protein) and a WPI-led blend (ON Gold Standard, 80% protein by weight) at equivalent caloric intake: fewer carbohydrates and fat per gram of protein delivered. For lactose-sensitive users — a meaningful subset of urban India, where lactase persistence is lower than in European populations — the near-zero lactose content of a true WPI matters. Cross-flow filtration at 91% protein typically leaves less than 1g of lactose per 34g scoop. See: WPI ingredient entry →

The April 2023 certification age — context you need

Trustified's April 2023 certification is now over three years old. This is the oldest active certification in our current India whey review series. Nutrabay Gold WPI carries a November 2024 Eurofins pass; Nakpro Gold (WPC) carries a 2023 Eurofins pass. Batch-level manufacturing can change. The April 2023 certification tells you the product was clean three years ago; it does not certify today's production. Nakpro has not published a newer batch-specific independent COA publicly. The certification remains "Active" on Trustified's site, which means Trustified has not rescinded it — but it should not be read as a rolling certification of current batches.

Who should buy this

Buy Nakpro Platinum if you
  • Want the cheapest independently verified WPI in India — no equivalent exists below ₹8/g with a Trustified pass
  • Are lactose-sensitive and need the near-zero lactose content of a true isolate (unflavoured: 91.2% protein, minimal residual lactose)
  • Add protein to oats, dahi, smoothies or cook with it — the unflavoured variant has no sweetener, cocoa, or lecithin
  • Are a recreational gym user in Bengaluru, Delhi, Pune, or Mumbai who wants a domestic brand with lab documentation and doesn't need imported brand prestige
  • Have been using ON Gold Standard and want to understand what ₹27.5/g vs ₹7.3/g actually buys you in terms of protein quality
Consider alternatives if you
  • Need Informed Choice UK anti-doping certification for WADA-governed competition — only ON Gold Standard carries this among reviewed India-market products
  • Want a more recent batch certification — Nutrabay Gold's November 2024 Eurofins pass is 18+ months newer than Nakpro's April 2023 cert
  • Prefer a pre-flavoured product with enzyme blend and GI support — Nakpro Platinum Plus+ addresses this, though per-gram cost rises to ~₹9.0
  • Are buying for a child or teenager — the 31g per scoop dose exceeds the per-meal protein recommendation for younger users; standard WPC options at lower doses are more appropriate

Frequently asked questions

Has Nakpro Platinum passed independent lab testing?
Yes. Trustified conducted a blind-purchase Eurofins test on Nakpro Platinum Whey Isolate, published 19 April 2023, status currently Active. The methodology: anonymous retail purchase, camera-recorded unboxing, Eurofins India NABL-accredited testing — no brand involvement. The full HPLC amino acid panel found no free amino acid spiking. Protein content matched the label claim. The certification is over three years old, which is worth noting relative to the newer Nutrabay Gold November 2024 Eurofins pass. Nakpro has not published a newer independent batch COA publicly at the time of this review.
What is the difference between Nakpro Platinum and Nakpro Platinum Plus+?
The core Platinum delivers 28g protein (flavoured) or 31g protein (unflavoured) per 34g scoop with nothing added beyond the WPI base, flavouring agents, and soy lecithin. Platinum Plus+ delivers 28g protein per 34g scoop with a digestive enzyme blend (Protease, Lactase, Amylase, Lipase), added vitamins and minerals (B-complex, Vitamin C, Vitamin D), and a higher price point (~₹9.0/g protein vs ~₹8.0/g). The enzyme and vitamin doses in Plus+ are not individually disclosed on the label. For most users who are not specifically targeting the enzyme or micronutrient additions, the core Platinum is the better value choice.
Is the unflavoured Nakpro Platinum a true isolate?
Yes. At 31g protein from a 34g scoop, the unflavoured Platinum delivers 91.2% protein by weight — above the 90% threshold used to define a true whey protein isolate. The flavoured variants at 28g per 34g scoop deliver 82.4% protein by weight, which is in the WPI-adjacent range but technically a blend by the strict greater-than-90% definition (the non-protein fraction is occupied by cocoa, flavouring, sweetener, soy lecithin). Both are produced from the same cross-flow microfiltered WPI base. The protein content difference is the flavour additions, not a different raw material.
How does Nakpro Platinum compare to ON Gold Standard?
ON Gold Standard is a WPI + WPC + hydrolysed whey blend delivering 24g protein per 30g scoop (80% by weight) at approximately ₹27.5/g of protein. It carries Informed Choice UK anti-doping certification and an Unbox Health A+ rating that expired in August 2025. Nakpro Platinum (unflavoured) delivers 91.2% protein by weight at ₹7.3/g — 3.7× cheaper per gram with a higher protein concentration. ON's meaningful advantages over Nakpro: Informed Choice UK certification (critical for drug-tested athletes), global brand infrastructure, and for buyers concerned about Nakpro's certification age, ON's brand recognition provides a different kind of assurance. For a recreational user without drug-testing obligations, the ₹20/g premium for ON is difficult to justify on quality grounds.

Full rubric — Naked Compound v3.0

1 · Evidence quality (ingredient) 9.0/10

Whey protein isolate has the deepest independent RCT evidence base in sports nutrition. Morton et al. 2018 (meta-analysis, 49 RCTs, 1,863 subjects) established protein supplementation significantly augments lean mass and strength gains from resistance training. Cermak et al. 2012 (meta-analysis) confirmed the post-exercise MPS augmentation. Leucine's mTORC1 activation pathway at 2–3g/dose is established at the molecular mechanism level (Norton et al. 2006, J Nutr) and confirmed in multiple human MPS RCTs. No free amino additions means the evidence applies to the actual ingredient rather than a nitrogen-diluted proxy. 1-point deduction: no cross-flow-specific vs ion-exchange WPI head-to-head RCT data in trained adults — the bioactive protein fraction preservation claim is mechanistically plausible but not clinically confirmed in outcome studies. Evidence tier: Strong (meta-analysis, RCT)2

2 · Ingredient form 9.0/10

Cross-flow microfiltered WPI at 91.2% protein by weight (unflavoured) is the correct form for a product positioned as a pure isolate. The unflavoured variant clears the 90% threshold definitively. Flavoured variants at 82.4% are in the functional isolate range but not true isolate by the strict definition — this is the consequence of flavouring agents and cocoa, not a different protein source. No magnesium stearate, no silicon dioxide, no microcrystalline cellulose. Soy lecithin (emulsifier, in flavoured variants) is disclosed and its dose is not clinically relevant. BCAA profile naturally occurring from whey base — not from separate amino acid additions. 1-point deduction: flavoured variants' 82.4% protein-by-weight means buyers paying for "100% Isolate" at ₹8/g get something technically in the concentrate-adjacent range by weight — though the raw material is isolate.

3 · Purity & manufacturing 8.5/10

Trustified Eurofins blind-purchase pass (April 2023, Active) is the correct form of independent verification — not brand-commissioned, not self-reported NABL. The Citizens Protein Project (Philips et al. 2024) found 69% of Indian protein supplements mislabelled; Nakpro Platinum is not in that list. Nakpro manufactures from imported global raw materials at their Bengaluru facility. FSSAI compliant. Non-GMO, Gluten-free, no banned substances claim. The 1.5-point deduction is calibrated against two specific gaps: (1) the April 2023 certification is now over three years old with no publicly available newer independent batch COA — legitimate batch quality can change; (2) no NSF, Informed Choice, or equivalent global anti-doping certification. These are meaningful gaps for drug-tested athletes and users who want continuous batch coverage. For the recreational user, neither gap is critical.

4 · Value for money 9.5/10

₹7.3/g protein (unflavoured) with an active Trustified Eurofins certification is, without qualification, the best value-per-documented-gram of WPI in the Indian market at the time of this review. The second-best documented option (Nutrabay Gold WPI at ₹9.6/g) costs 31% more for a product with a newer certification but lower protein-per-100g (83.3% vs 91.2%). ON Gold Standard at ₹27.5/g costs 3.76× more for a WPI-led blend that tops out at 80% protein by weight. The D2C distribution reduces counterfeiting risk that affects ON and UN ISO Sensation significantly. 0.5-point deduction from maximum because the Plus+ and ISO Zero SKUs dilute the value story for buyers who accidentally purchase the wrong variant.

5 · Label honesty 8.0/10

The core supplement facts panel is accurate — protein content is confirmed by Eurofins, no free amino additions, ingredient disclosure is complete. 2-point deduction for two labelling issues that are real. First: the "100% Whey Isolate" label on the flavoured variants is technically accurate (the protein source is 100% isolate) but creates a reasonable expectation of 90%+ protein by weight that the flavoured product does not deliver at 82.4%. This is a common industry convention, not unique to Nakpro, but it is still a misleading frame. Second: the Nakpro Platinum product family is so fragmented — standard, unflavoured, Plus+, ISO Zero, with Creatine, Platinum+ — with similar packaging and naming, that distinguishing between them requires careful reading at purchase time. Branding clarity is a component of label honesty, and the Nakpro Platinum family fails this specific test.
