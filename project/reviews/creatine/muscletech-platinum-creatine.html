The bottom line

Naked Compound verdict · 7.2 / 10

Muscletech's Creapure claim is historical. The Indian import product lacks the Creapure mark and active batch certification. At ₹17.5/dose, MuscleBlaze CreaPRO at ₹15.0 with an explicit Creapure logo is the better call.

Muscletech has a long supplement history — founded in Canada in 1995, acquired by Iovate Health Sciences, and sold across India through import distribution. Their Platinum Creatine was among the first supplements in the Indian gym market to gain broad awareness, partly through Muscletech's heavy sponsorship presence in domestic bodybuilding circles through the 2000s–2010s.

The historical sourcing claim for Muscletech Platinum Creatine involved Creapure from AlzChem, Germany. However, the Indian import product available in 2025–2026 does not carry the Creapure certification mark on the label — at least not consistently across the units we inspected. The AlzChem Creapure verification system requires active licensing, and brands must display the mark to indicate active certification. Without the mark, the Creapure sourcing claim cannot be independently verified through the AlzChem check system.

This puts Platinum Creatine in the same documentation position as other import creatines without active certification: the brand quality is real, the monohydrate form is correct, the dose is right — but the independent verification layer that would distinguish it from lower-tier domestic products is absent. MuscleBlaze CreaPRO, which explicitly carries the Creapure mark on its Indian label and is domestically distributed, is the better buy at ₹15.0/dose vs ₹17.5.

Why the Creapure mark matters — and why its absence does not

Creapure is a registered trademark of AlzChem Trostberg GmbH, Germany. The Creapure logo indicates that the creatine monohydrate raw material in the product was manufactured at AlzChem's Trostberg facility to their specification: ≥99.9% creatine monohydrate, ≤60 ppm creatinine, ≤50 ppm dicyandiamide, ≤3 ppm dihydrotriazine, and ≤10 mg/kg heavy metals.

The verification process: consumers and professionals can verify Creapure certification by entering the product name at the official AlzChem Creapure database. A certified product appears in their registry. An uncertified product — even one historically sourced from AlzChem — does not.

How to check Creapure certification for any product

Visit the AlzChem Creapure supplier search at creapure.com. Enter the brand name. If the product appears, the current batch is Creapure-licensed. If it does not appear, either the product is not Creapure-sourced, or the brand's licence has lapsed. This check takes 30 seconds and is the most reliable way to verify Creapure status without buying a product first.

The absence of the Creapure mark from an Indian product's label does not mean the creatine inside is inferior — there are many high-quality monohydrate manufacturers globally. It means you cannot verify the specific source claim through AlzChem's public database. The quality could be equal to Creapure; you have no way to confirm it independently.

Label analysis

Muscletech Platinum 100% Creatine (400g unflavoured) contains: creatine monohydrate (micronized). Single ingredient. No fillers, no flowing agents in the unflavoured format. The 5g scoop is correctly calibrated. The "Platinum" and "100%" brand language implies purity — which is the standard positioning for single-ingredient sports supplements. The label does not carry a Creapure certification mark.

Muscletech India imports through local distributors. The Indian label must carry FSSAI registration and importer details — verify these are present on any unit you purchase, as grey market Muscletech product without proper Indian regulatory markings has been documented on third-party marketplace channels.

Value: the import premium problem

At ₹1,399 for 400g (80 doses), the per-dose cost is ₹17.5. This is 17% more than MuscleBlaze CreaPRO (₹15.0, explicit Creapure mark) and nearly 3× more than AS-IT-IS (₹9.2, NABL COA). The premium over both alternatives is not supported by superior documentation — it reflects import logistics and distributor margin, not quality difference.

Who this is for

Consider only if: You have bought Muscletech Platinum before, had a good experience, and have a strong brand loyalty to Muscletech's existing products. The ingredient itself is fine — the documentation gap is the concern, not a formulation problem.

Not recommended as a first-time creatine purchase. MuscleBlaze CreaPRO (₹15/dose, explicit Creapure mark) or AS-IT-IS (₹9.2/dose, NABL COA) provide more independently verifiable quality at lower cost.
