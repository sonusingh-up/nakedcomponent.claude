The bottom line

Naked Compound verdict · 7.9 / 10

Creapure-certified at ₹15/dose — the best price-to-supply-chain ratio for documented creatine in India. The score penalty is for label language that inflates the product's identity.

MuscleBlaze CreaPRO delivers exactly what creatine supplementation requires: Creapure-certified monohydrate at 5g per scoop, from a brand with broad HealthKart distribution and enough market scale to face accountability for systematic quality failures. The "PRO" label and associated marketing language around it are where the score gets dented — not the chemistry.

The honest comparison: GNC Pro Performance Creatine uses the same Creapure raw material at 5g per scoop and costs ₹21.7/dose. MuscleBlaze CreaPRO delivers identical Creapure chemistry at ₹15/dose — a ₹6.7 saving per serving with no molecular trade-off. This is the rational Creapure choice for the Indian market unless you specifically need GNC's physical retail footprint or formal anti-doping documentation.

What Creapure certification actually means

Creapure is a registered trademark of AlzChem AG (Trostberg, Germany). AlzChem's creatine synthesis uses a patented process producing creatine monohydrate at ≥99.9% purity, with manufacturing to pharmaceutical-grade standards. The certification covers the raw material at the point of German synthesis — including content accuracy, creatinine ceiling (<0.1%), absence of dihydrotriazine and other synthesis byproducts, and traceability through the supply chain.1

AlzChem maintains a public database of licensed Creapure users. MuscleBlaze's CreaPRO line is on it as of the last verified check (May 2026). This is verifiable by consumers directly at creapure.com. What Creapure certification does not cover: finished-product integrity after the raw material leaves Germany, finished-product testing in India, or batch-level QC by the Indian manufacturer. These gaps are why the purity sub-score doesn't reach 9+.

Why Creapure brands don't publish batch COAs

AlzChem provides Creapure licensees with its own certification documentation rather than raw material COAs. Indian supplement brands using Creapure can legally cite the AlzChem certification without publishing separate batch COAs — which is why most Creapure brands in India (including MuscleBlaze) don't publish batch-level analytical reports. This is a structural gap in Creapure's India consumer transparency, not necessarily evidence of poor quality. It's the one advantage NABL-documented domestic brands like AS-IT-IS hold over the Creapure field.

The CreaPRO HMB variant — avoid it

MuscleBlaze also sells a "CreaPRO with HMB" variant at approximately 2× the price per dose of standard CreaPRO. HMB (β-hydroxy β-methylbutyrate) has a plausible anti-catabolic mechanism and some positive trial data — but its evidence base is significantly weaker and more contested than creatine monohydrate, and the doses used in positive HMB trials (3g/day) are generally not met by combination creatine-HMB products. You are paying a substantial premium for a second ingredient at likely sub-effective dosing.

The standard CreaPRO (creatine monohydrate only) is the product reviewed here and the one we recommend. The HMB variant is not supported by a cost-benefit case that holds up to evidence scrutiny.

⚠
MuscleBlaze "PRO" naming — what it does and doesn't mean

MuscleBlaze's "PRO" line implies professional-grade formulation differentiation. For CreaPRO, the practical differentiation is the Creapure supply source — which is real and meaningful. But the word "PRO" should not be read to imply that this creatine outperforms other Creapure-sourced products in any technical sense. GNC Pro Performance Creatine uses the same Creapure raw material. "PRO" is a product tier name, not a biochemistry claim. The label honesty score reflects this naming inflation.

Dosing — correctly delivered

The serving is 5g creatine monohydrate. This is the correct maintenance dose per the ISSN position stand (Kreider et al., 2017) and the Buford et al. meta-analysis — 3–5g/day for maintenance, with optional loading at 20g/day for 5–7 days to saturate faster.2 At 5g per scoop, four MuscleBlaze CreaPRO scoops equal the standard loading dose. The dose is right. There is no mystery ingredient, no proprietary blend element, and no under-dosing — which is notable in the MuscleBlaze range, where some products use proprietary blends.
