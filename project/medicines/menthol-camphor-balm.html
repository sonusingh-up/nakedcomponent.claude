What it's for

Uses

Blocked nose comfort Cough-y nights Tension headache (temples) Steam inhalation add-in Muscle-rub warmth

Why it earns its place

Benefits

  • Instant sensory relief that helps sleep through a cold
  • No tablets, no interactions with other medicines
  • One tin serves the whole family's adults for months

The honest column

Side effects

Common — usually settle

  • Skin irritation or burning on sensitive skin
  • Watery eyes from fumes

Stop & see a doctor

  • Camphor is poisonous if swallowed — keep away from children
  • On or under an infant's nostrils it can trigger breathing distress — never do this

Before you take it

Cautions & interactions

  • Not for children under 2 at all; for older children, chest and back only
  • Never inside the nose, on broken skin, or near the eyes
  • It comforts symptoms — it doesn't shorten the cold; persistent cough needs a doctor

When to skip self-medication

Fever beyond 3 days, blood anywhere it shouldn't be, chest pain or breathlessness, severe abdominal pain, a child under 6, pregnancy, or symptoms bouncing back within a week — any of these means see a doctor the same day instead of reaching for this pack.
