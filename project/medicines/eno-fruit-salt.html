Fruit salt (ENO)

Sodium bicarbonate + citric acid + sodium carbonate

Instant fizz for occasional heartburn — with a sodium load your blood pressure notices.

FormPowder sachet
Adult dose*1 sachet (5 g) in water
FrequencyAs needed, occasionally
Works in2–6 min
!

Educational reference only — not medical advice. *Doses shown are typical adult label doses. Read the pack, and confirm with a doctor or pharmacist — especially for children, pregnancy, the elderly, or any chronic condition.

What it's for

Uses

Sudden heartburn Acidity after heavy meals Bloated, over-full feeling

Why it earns its place

Benefits

  • Among the fastest OTC acidity fixes — relief in minutes
  • Single-use sachets travel well
  • No drowsiness, no interactions with most medicines at occasional use

The honest column

Side effects

Common — usually settle

  • Belching
  • Temporary bloating
  • Salty aftertaste

Stop & see a doctor

  • Regular use adds significant sodium — risky with high BP, heart or kidney disease
  • Swelling of feet or sudden BP spikes with frequent use — stop and see a doctor

Before you take it

Cautions & interactions

  • It's a rescue, not a routine — daily acidity needs a doctor, not daily ENO
  • On a salt-restricted diet (BP, heart, kidney, pregnancy-induced hypertension)? Ask first
  • Wait for the fizz to settle before drinking to avoid gulping gas

When to skip self-medication

Fever beyond 3 days, blood anywhere it shouldn't be, chest pain or breathlessness, severe abdominal pain, a child under 6, pregnancy, or symptoms bouncing back within a week — any of these means see a doctor the same day instead of reaching for this pack.
