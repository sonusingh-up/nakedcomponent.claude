What we keep on this list after the lightning deal ends — and how to tell a sustained bestseller from a one-day spike.

Frequently asked
Does bestseller rank mean a product is genuinely good?
Not by itself. Bestseller rank reflects sales velocity, which can be driven by aggressive discounting or a lightning deal as easily as by genuine quality. We only keep a product on this list if sustained rank is backed by review authenticity and ingredient or formulation claims that hold up independently.
How often does a bestseller list actually change?
Amazon's bestseller rank recalculates continuously and can shift within hours during a sale event. A product's rank on any single day tells you less than its rank trend over weeks or months — sustained position is the more meaningful signal.
Can brands pay to appear higher in bestseller rankings?
Brands can't directly buy bestseller rank, but they can influence the sales volume that drives it — through ad spend, deep discounting, or coordinated promotional pushes — which is exactly why a single-day rank spike is weaker evidence than a rank sustained across months.
Are the reviews behind a bestseller trustworthy?
Review count alone doesn't guarantee authenticity. Verified-purchase reviews, review velocity that matches organic sales patterns rather than a sudden spike, and detailed reviews rather than one-line ratings are better signals than total review count by itself.
