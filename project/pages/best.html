Vitamin D3 + K2 — ingredient deep view
↓
D3 vs D2
Cholecalciferol only
D3 is 87% more potent at raising 25-OH-D3 levels vs D2 and has 2× longer half-life (Tripkovic et al. 2012, meta-analysis). Always choose D3 (cholecalciferol).
K2: MK-7 vs MK-4
MK-7 at 100–200mcg/day
MK-7: 3-day half-life. MK-4: 1–4h. Indian supplement MK-4 doses (15–45mcg) are far below the 45–90mg/day used in Japanese pharmacological trials.
Evidence tier
RCTMeta-analysis
D3: robust RCT evidence for bone, immune function, and reduced respiratory infection risk (Martineau et al. 2017). D3+K2 for vascular calcification: Knapen et al. (2015), 3-yr RCT.
Mechanism
VDR signalling + MGP carboxylation
D3 → calcitriol → VDR → upregulates MGP and osteocalcin. Both require K2 for gamma-carboxylation. Without K2, MGP remains inactive and calcium may deposit in arteries.
India context
FSSAI Schedule II
Melanin reduces cutaneous D3 synthesis by up to 6×. Indoor urban workers, covered women, and elderly are highest risk. NFHS-5: >70% urban adults insufficient (<30 ng/mL).
Testing note
Test before mega-dosing
Check serum 25-OH-D before supplementing at 5,000 IU. Target: 40–60 ng/mL. Toxicity threshold: >150 ng/mL (rare at <10,000 IU/day). Retest after 3–4 months.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Is Creapure creatine worth the price premium in India?
Yes, for most Indian buyers. Creapure is produced by AlzChem in Germany to pharmaceutical-grade purity (99.9% creatine monohydrate, <10 ppm creatinine, <10 ppm dicyandiamide). Generic Chinese creatine is typically 99.0–99.5% pure — a small absolute difference, but the gap in quality control and batch-level consistency is meaningful. The price premium on Indian Creapure brands (BigMuscles, Nakpro) is ~20–30% over generic, which is justified given the COA transparency.
Why do most Indian omega-3 products under-deliver on EPA+DHA?
Indian labelling convention allows brands to display total fish oil weight (e.g., "1000mg") as the headline claim rather than EPA+DHA content. A standard 1000mg fish oil concentrate capsule contains only ~300mg EPA+DHA. Clinical benefit for triglyceride reduction requires 2–4g EPA+DHA per day — meaning 7–13 standard capsules. Always read the per-capsule EPA+DHA breakdown, not the total fish oil weight. Look for products declaring at least 500mg EPA+DHA per capsule.
What does FSSAI certification actually mean for supplements?
FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) certification confirms that a product is registered under the Food Safety and Standards (Health Supplements, Nutraceuticals) Regulations 2022, manufactured under GMP-compliant conditions, and labelled according to FSSAI standards. It is a manufacturing and labelling compliance standard — it does not verify potency claims, test individual batches, or confirm ingredient purity. FSSAI is necessary but insufficient for quality assurance. Independent COA testing matters more.
What is the difference between KSM-66 and generic ashwagandha?
KSM-66 is a patented, full-spectrum ashwagandha root extract from Ixoreal Biomed, standardised to ≥5% withanolides using a water-based extraction process that preserves the natural root profile. It has 24+ published RCTs. Generic ashwagandha is typically unlicensed Withania somnifera root/leaf powder or extract with unverified withanolide content. The clinical evidence from KSM-66 trials is not transferable to unlicensed extracts — different extraction methods produce different phytochemical profiles and potency.
Which form of magnesium should I buy in India?
Magnesium glycinate or bisglycinate. Most Indian products use magnesium oxide, which has approximately 4% fractional bioavailability (Firoz & Graber, 2001) and causes loose stools at higher doses. Glycinate form absorbs at ~80% and is well-tolerated at therapeutic doses. Check the supplement facts panel for elemental magnesium content — not the total compound weight. A 400mg magnesium oxide capsule delivers roughly 16mg of absorbable magnesium.
Do I need vitamin K2 alongside vitamin D3?
Yes, at supplemental doses above 2,000 IU D3 daily. Vitamin D3 upregulates two K2-dependent proteins — Matrix Gla Protein (MGP) and osteocalcin — which require K2 (MK-7 form) for activation via gamma-carboxylation. Without adequate K2, MGP remains inactive and calcium mobilised by D3 may deposit in soft tissues rather than bone. Use MK-7 at 100mcg/day. MK-4 at oral doses available in Indian supplements (<100mcg) is pharmacokinetically insufficient due to its short half-life.
