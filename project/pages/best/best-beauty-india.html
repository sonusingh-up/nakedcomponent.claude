Methodology

How this
edit works

This page applies the same filter Naked Compound uses on supplements — verified pricing, label-claim literacy, and no paid placement — to seven beauty categories. Every "why we picked this" is a real editorial judgement, not a sponsored slot.

Three checks run on everything before it earns a place in the edit: who the listing is sold by, whether the headline claim (SPF, active %, EDT/EDP) is actually substantiated on the label, and whether the price reflects real value once size and concentration are normalised.

Frequently asked
Is luxury beauty bought online actually authentic?
Only if the listing is sold directly by the brand or by a brand-authorised seller, shown under the "Sold by" line on the product page. Unauthorised third-party sellers are the highest-risk source for counterfeit fragrance and skincare online — check the seller before the price, not after.
What does SPF 50 PA++++ actually mean?
SPF measures UVB protection (sunburn risk); PA measures UVA protection (pigmentation, photoageing) on a scale from PA+ to PA++++. Indian dermatology guidance generally recommends SPF 50 with at least PA+++ year-round, since UVA-driven pigmentation and melasma are a near-constant risk across most of India.
EDT vs EDP — does it matter which one I buy?
Yes. Eau de Parfum concentrations (roughly 15–20% aromatic compounds) last longer and project further than Eau de Toilette (5–15%). Listings that only say "Perfume" or "Fragrance," without EDT, EDP or Cologne stated anywhere, are the most common source of disappointment on marketplace fragrance.
Does CDSCO regulate cosmetics the way FSSAI regulates supplements?
Cosmetics fall under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act via CDSCO — a manufacturing and import compliance framework, not a clinical-efficacy gatekeeper. A CDSCO-compliant label confirms a safety baseline; it doesn't independently verify a "brightening" or "anti-hairfall" claim.
Why do trimmer runtime claims vary so much from the box?
Runtime is measured in lab conditions at a fixed speed setting on a fresh battery. Real use on higher speed settings, with denser facial hair, and after battery ageing typically runs shorter than the stated figure. Checking charge time alongside runtime is more useful day-to-day than the runtime number alone.
