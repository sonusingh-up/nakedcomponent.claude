Frequently asked
Is Luxury Beauty on Amazon India actually authentic?
Only if the listing is sold or fulfilled by Amazon, or by a brand-authorised seller shown under "Sold by" on the product page. Unauthorised third-party sellers are the highest-risk source for counterfeit fragrance and skincare on Indian marketplaces — check the seller before the price, not after.
EDT vs EDP — does it matter which one I buy?
Yes. Eau de Parfum concentrations (typically 15–20% aromatic compounds) last longer and project further than Eau de Toilette (5–15%). Listings that only say "Perfume" or "Fragrance," without EDT, EDP or Cologne stated anywhere, are the most common source of disappointment on marketplace fragrance.
Does CDSCO regulate cosmetics the way FSSAI regulates supplements?
Cosmetics fall under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act via CDSCO — a manufacturing and import compliance framework, not a clinical-efficacy gatekeeper. A CDSCO-compliant label confirms a safety baseline; it doesn't prove a "brightening" or "anti-ageing" claim.
Why are the prices on this page placeholders?
This page is a layout prototype. The editorial structure, imagery slots, and card system are production-ready — but the specific products, prices, and stats shown are illustrative until real picks and verified pricing are swapped in.
