What is RSS?

RSS is a standard web format that lets you follow any site without visiting it. A feed reader (like Feedly, NetNewsWire, or Reeder) checks our feed URL and shows you new content alongside everything else you follow — no social-media noise, no algorithmic reordering.

How to subscribe

Option 1 — Use a dedicated feed reader

Copy a feed URL above and paste it into any feed reader. Popular choices:

  • Feedly (feedly.com) — web & mobile, free tier available
  • NetNewsWire — free, open-source, Mac & iOS
  • Reeder — Mac & iOS, paid
  • NewsBlur (newsblur.com) — web & mobile, free & paid tiers
  • Miniflux — self-hosted, for those who prefer full control

Option 2 — Subscribe in your browser

Firefox and Safari support RSS natively. Paste the feed URL into the address bar and click "Subscribe." For Chrome, install an extension like "RSS Feed Reader" or "Feedbro" first.

Option 3 — Receive updates by email via a feed-to-email service

Services like Blogtrottr or Kill the Newsletter convert any RSS feed into email digests. Paste our feed URL into the service and get updates in your inbox without giving us your address.

Technical details

All feeds conform to the RSS 2.0 specification. Each item includes a full title, publication date, summary description, category tags, and a canonical link to the full article. Feeds are updated within 15 minutes of a new publication going live.

If you notice a feed error — malformed XML, missing items, or stale data — please report it at tech@nakedcompound.in with the feed URL and the issue.
