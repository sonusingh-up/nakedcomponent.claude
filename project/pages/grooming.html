Runtime claims are a lab number.

What actually holds up on a real beard, on a real charge — trimmers, beard oil, and face wash, checked past the box copy.

By the Naked Compound Research Team· 5 min read· Updated September 2026

A trimmer's "90-minute runtime" is measured under lab conditions, at a fixed speed setting, on a fresh battery. Real-world use on a higher speed setting, against denser facial hair, and after months of charge cycles typically runs shorter — which makes charge time, not runtime alone, the more useful number to check before buying.

We read grooming listings for what the spec sheet actually supports — blade material, charge time, warranty terms — over what the front-of-pack claims. Three picks, and the checks worth running on anything else.

Frequently asked
Why do trimmer runtime claims vary so much from the box?
Runtime is measured in lab conditions at a fixed speed setting on a fresh battery. Real use on higher speed settings, with denser facial hair, and after battery ageing typically runs shorter than the stated figure. Checking charge time alongside runtime is more useful day-to-day than the runtime number alone.
Does more blade guards mean a better trimmer?
Not necessarily past a practical range. A 10-to-20 length range covers most use cases; guard count matters less than blade material (titanium or stainless steel resists dulling better) and whether the blade module is self-sharpening or replaceable.
Is an "Ayurvedic" or "natural" claim on a grooming product regulated?
Cosmetic-category grooming products fall under CDSCO's manufacturing and import compliance framework, the same as any other cosmetic — "Ayurvedic" or "natural" on the front of pack is a marketing descriptor, not an independently certified claim, unless the product carries specific AYUSH licensing separately.
What warranty support should I expect on a trimmer bought online?
Established brands with an India service-centre network (Philips, for instance) typically offer 1-2 year warranties honourable at physical service centres, not just online replacement. Checking for a named India warranty period on the listing, not just "warranty included," is worth doing before buying.
