Creatine monohydrate — ingredient deep view
↓
Active compound
Creatine monohydrate
Not HCl, ethyl ester, or "buffered" — monohydrate has the broadest RCT support of any creatine form.
Clinical dose range
3–5 g/day
Maintenance. Loading protocol: 20g/day × 5 days (optional; reaches saturation ~30% faster).
Evidence tier
RCTMeta-analysis

Mechanism
PCr → ATP resynthesis

India context
FSSAI Schedule II

Key citation
Lanhers et al., 2017
Meta-analysis, 22 RCTs. Upper body strength: +3.7% vs placebo. Eur J Sport Sci. Note: Not industry sponsored.

EPA + DHA — ingredient deep view
↓
Active compounds
EPA + DHA (marine)
Not ALA (flaxseed). ALA-to-EPA conversion in humans is <10%; ALA does not substitute for EPA+DHA in clinical endpoints.
Clinical dose
1–4 g/day
EPA+DHA combined. 2–4g/day for triglyceride reduction (RCT-level). 1g/day for general CVD risk (observational).
Evidence tier
RCTMeta-analysis
REDUCE-IT (2018, Bhatt et al.) — 25% reduction in MACE with 4g EPA-only (icosapentaenoic acid) in high-risk patients. STRENGTH trial found no benefit with DHA-rich formulations — the EPA:DHA ratio matters.
Mechanism
eicosanoid modulation
EPA/DHA compete with arachidonic acid for COX/LOX enzymes, shifting prostaglandin production toward less inflammatory series-3 eicosanoids. DHA preferentially concentrates in neural tissue.
India context
FSSAI Schedule II
Inland vegetarian populations in India (UP, Rajasthan, Gujarat) average 20–40mg dietary EPA+DHA per day — near zero. Coastal populations (Kerala, Goa, Bengal) average 400–600mg. Supplementation impact is highest for vegetarians and inland urban populations.
Key citation
Bhatt et al., 2018
REDUCE-IT trial. 4g/day icosapentaenoic acid ethyl ester. 25% ↓ MACE vs placebo. NEJM. Industry sponsored (Amarin). Note: mineral oil placebo used — ongoing debate.

Whey Protein Isolate — ingredient deep view
↓
Form distinction
WPI vs WPC
WPI: ≥90% protein by dry weight, <1% lactose, <1% fat. WPC: 70–80% protein, 3–8% lactose. Concentrate is adequate for most; isolate is indicated for lactose sensitivity (~30% of Indians).
Clinical dose
20–40g/serving
To maximise MPS: 0.3–0.4g/kg per meal (20–35g for most). Total daily target: 1.6–2.2g/kg (ISSN 2017). Distribute across 3–4 meals.
Evidence tier
RCTMeta-analysis
Morton et al., 2018 meta-analysis (49 studies, n=1,800): protein supplementation significantly increased 1RM strength and lean mass when combined with resistance training. Effect size modest but robust.
Mechanism
Leucine → mTORC1
Leucine content is the primary driver of MPS. Whey's leucine (~11%) exceeds casein (~9%) and soy (~7.5%), triggering mTORC1 signalling more acutely.
India context
FSSAI Schedule II
India has a documented dietary protein gap, particularly in vegetarian populations eating predominantly cereal-based diets (dal-rice, roti-sabzi). NFHS-5 data indicates widespread protein inadequacy in women aged 15–49.
Red flag
Nitrogen spiking
Addition of cheap amino acids (glycine, taurine) or creatine to inflate crude protein readings on Kjeldahl nitrogen testing. Amino acid-specific testing (HPLC) is the only reliable method. Always ask for full amino acid panel COA.

Ashwagandha — ingredient deep view
↓
Active compounds
Withanolides (steroidal lactones)
Primary bioactives: withaferin A, withanolide D. Modulate HPA axis, reduce cortisol, exhibit GABA-mimetic and thyroid-modulating activity.
KSM-66 dose
300–600mg/day
600mg/day is the most-studied dose. Lower 300mg doses used in some sleep trials (Deshpande et al., 2020).
Evidence tier
RCTMeta-analysis
Pratte et al. meta-analysis (2014): KSM-66 reduced cortisol by 14–32% vs placebo in stressed adults across 5 RCTs. Chandrasekhar et al. (2012) is the landmark stress/cortisol RCT.

HPA axis modulation

India context
FSSAI Schedule II

Liver signal (rare)

Magnesium — ingredient deep view
↓
Form hierarchy
Glycinate > Malate > Citrate > Oxide
Glycinate/bisglycinate: highest bioavailability, no laxative effect. Malate: good absorption, better for energy (Krebs cycle). Citrate: moderate, mild laxative. Oxide: poor absorption, strong laxative — not appropriate as primary form.
Clinical dose
300–400mg/day
Elemental magnesium, not compound weight. A 400mg bisglycinate capsule contains ~50–60mg elemental magnesium — read the label.
Evidence tier
RCTMeta-analysis
Zhang et al. (2016) meta-analysis: magnesium supplementation significantly reduced blood pressure in prehypertensive adults. Abbasi et al. (2012): improved sleep efficiency with 500mg daily in elderly individuals.
Mechanism
NMDA antagonism + ATP cofactor
Cofactor in 300+ enzymatic reactions. Blocks NMDA glutamate receptors at physiological concentrations — mechanism behind sleep and anxiolytic effects. Required for ATP synthesis and muscle contraction/relaxation.
India context
Dietary gap common
Cereal-based Indian diets are high in phytates, which bind magnesium and reduce absorption. Estimated 48% of urban Indians are magnesium insufficient based on dietary surveys. Processed food consumption further reduces magnesium intake in metro populations.
Key citation
Firoz & Graber, 2001
Head-to-head bioavailability study: oxide 4%, citrate 66%, glycinate ~80% relative to reference dose. Am J Ther. The foundational bioavailability comparison still cited by formulators.

Vitamin D3 + K2 — ingredient deep view
↓
D3 vs D2
Cholecalciferol (D3) only
D3 is 87% more potent at raising 25-OH-D3 serum levels vs D2 (ergocalciferol) and has a 2× longer half-life. Always choose D3. (Tripkovic et al., 2012, meta-analysis).
K2: MK-7 vs MK-4
MK-7 at 100–200mcg/day
MK-7 has 3-day half-life vs 1–4h for MK-4. Indian oral MK-4 doses (15–45mcg) are far below the 45–90mg/day shown effective in Japanese MK-4 trials (which used pharmacological, not nutritional, dosing).
Evidence tier
RCTMeta-analysis
Vitamin D: robust RCT evidence for bone mineralisation, immune function, and reduction in respiratory infection risk (Martineau et al., 2017). K2+D3 combination for vascular calcification: Knapen et al. (2015) — 3yr RCT.
Mechanism
VDR signalling + MGP carboxylation
D3 → 1,25-OH-D3 (calcitriol) → VDR → upregulates MGP and osteocalcin → both require K2 for carboxylation activation. Without K2, MGP remains inactive and calcium may deposit in arteries.
India context
FSSAI Schedule II
Despite abundant sunlight, India has paradoxically high vitamin D deficiency. Melanin in darker skin reduces cutaneous D3 synthesis by up to 6×. Urban indoor workers, women in full-body cover, and elderly are highest risk. NFHS-5: >70% urban adults insufficient.
Testing note
Test before mega-dosing
Check serum 25-OH-D before supplementing at 5,000 IU. Target: 40–60 ng/mL. At 2,000 IU/day, most deficient adults reach sufficiency in 3–4 months. Toxicity threshold: >150 ng/mL (rare at doses <10,000 IU).

Frequently asked

Is Creapure creatine worth the price premium in India?
Yes, for most Indian buyers. Creapure is produced by AlzChem in Germany to pharmaceutical-grade purity (99.9% creatine monohydrate, <10 ppm creatinine, <10 ppm dicyandiamide). Generic Chinese creatine is typically 99.0–99.5% pure — a small difference in absolute terms, but the gap in quality control and consistency is meaningful. Indian Creapure brands (BigMuscles, Nakpro) carry a ~20–30% price premium over generic, which is justified given the COA transparency.
Why do most Indian omega-3 products under-deliver on EPA+DHA?
Indian labelling convention allows brands to display total fish oil weight (e.g., "1000mg") as the headline claim rather than EPA+DHA content. A standard 1000mg fish oil concentrate capsule contains only ~300mg EPA+DHA. Clinical benefit for triglyceride reduction requires 2–4g EPA+DHA per day — meaning 7–13 standard capsules. Always read the per-capsule EPA+DHA breakdown panel, not the fish oil weight. Look for products declaring at least 500mg EPA+DHA per capsule.
What does FSSAI certification actually mean for supplements?
FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) certification confirms that a product is registered under the Food Safety and Standards (Health Supplements, Nutraceuticals) Regulations 2022, manufactured under GMP-compliant conditions, and labelled according to FSSAI standards. It is a manufacturing and labelling compliance standard — it does not verify potency claims, test individual batches, or confirm ingredient purity. FSSAI is necessary but insufficient for quality assurance. Independent COA testing matters more.
What is the difference between KSM-66 and generic ashwagandha?
KSM-66 is a patented, full-spectrum ashwagandha root extract from Ixoreal Biomed, standardised to ≥5% withanolides using a water-based extraction process that preserves the natural root profile. It has 24+ published RCTs. Generic ashwagandha is typically unlicensed Withania somnifera root/leaf powder or extract with unverified withanolide content. Which form of magnesium should I buy in India?
Magnesium glycinate or bisglycinate. Most Indian products use magnesium oxide, which has approximately 4% fractional bioavailability (Firoz & Graber, 2001) and causes loose stools at higher doses due to osmotic laxative effect. Glycinate form absorbs at ~80% and is well-tolerated at therapeutic doses (300–400mg elemental/day). Check the supplement facts panel for elemental magnesium content — not the total compound weight. A 400mg magnesium oxide capsule delivers roughly 16mg of absorbable magnesium.
Do I need vitamin K2 alongside vitamin D3?
Yes, at supplemental doses above 2,000 IU D3 daily. Vitamin D3 upregulates two K2-dependent proteins — Matrix Gla Protein (MGP) and osteocalcin — which require K2 (MK-7 form) for activation via gamma-carboxylation. Without adequate K2, MGP remains inactive and calcium mobilised by D3 may deposit in soft tissues (arteries, kidneys) rather than bone. Use MK-7 at 100mcg/day; MK-4 at oral doses available in Indian supplements (<100mcg) is pharmacokinetically insufficient due to its short half-life.
