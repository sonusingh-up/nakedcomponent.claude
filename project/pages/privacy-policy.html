1. Who we are

Naked Compound ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website nakedcompound.in — an independent editorial platform covering health supplements, nutrition, and beauty products for an Indian audience. We are based in India.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, we are a Data Fiduciary — the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing your personal data. You, the individual whose data we process, are the Data Principal.

For visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, we are the Data Controller under the GDPR/UK GDPR.

For questions about this policy or to exercise your rights, contact us at naked.business@yahoo.com.

4. Legal basis for processing

Under both the DPDP Act 2023 and the GDPR, we process personal data only where a lawful basis exists:

  • Consent: You have given clear, specific, and informed consent — for example, by subscribing to our newsletter. You may withdraw consent at any time without penalty (see Your rights).
  • Legitimate interests: We have a legitimate interest in understanding how our site is used and in maintaining its security. These interests are weighed against your rights and we process only the minimum data necessary.
  • Legal obligation: Where we are required by Indian or applicable international law to process or retain data.

We do not process any special category data (as defined under GDPR Art. 9) or sensitive personal data (as defined under DPDP Act 2023).

11. India: DPDP Act 2023

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 ("DPDP Act") is India's primary personal data protection law. This section explains how we comply with its requirements.

Consent notice

Under the DPDP Act, we are required to provide a clear consent notice before collecting your personal data. By subscribing to our newsletter or submitting the contact form, you are providing free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent to the processing described in this policy. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

Data Fiduciary obligations

As a Data Fiduciary, we commit to:

  • Collecting only the data necessary for the stated purpose (data minimisation)
  • Not retaining data beyond the period necessary for the stated purpose
  • Implementing reasonable security safeguards
  • Notifying you and the Data Protection Board of India in the event of a personal data breach that is likely to affect your interests
  • Establishing a Grievance Redressal Mechanism (see Contact & grievances)

Children's data (DPDP Act)

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the age of 18. If we become aware that a child has provided data without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it promptly. Our site does not offer services directed at children. (See also Section 13.)

Cross-border transfers

We may transfer personal data to countries outside India through our use of Google Analytics and our newsletter provider. We ensure that any such transfer is subject to adequate safeguards as required under the DPDP Act and any applicable rules notified by the Indian Government.

Grievance redressal (DPDP Act)

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to have your grievance addressed by us before approaching the Data Protection Board of India. To raise a grievance, contact us at naked.business@yahoo.com with the subject line "DPDP Grievance." We will acknowledge your grievance within 48 hours and resolve it within 30 days.

If your grievance is not resolved to your satisfaction, you may escalate it to the Data Protection Board of India at dpb.gov.in.

13. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at naked.business@yahoo.com and we will promptly delete the data.

Under the DPDP Act 2023, we are required to obtain verifiable parental consent before processing the personal data of any child. We do not have the infrastructure to verify age or obtain parental consent, and therefore our services are available to adults only. If we discover that a minor has subscribed, we will delete the data and the subscription.
