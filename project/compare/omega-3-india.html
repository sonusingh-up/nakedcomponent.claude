Frequently asked questions

What is the best omega-3 supplement in India in 2026?

Nordic Naturals Ultimate Omega (score 9.0) — IFOS 5-star certified, high EPA+DHA concentration, minimal oxidation. Best domestic option: Carbamide Forte Triple Strength (score 8.6) — IFOS certified, 550mg EPA+330mg DHA per capsule at ₹449/60ct.

What does 1000mg fish oil actually mean in terms of omega-3?

A standard 1000mg fish oil capsule contains approximately 180mg EPA + 120mg DHA = 300mg total omega-3. To reach the cardiovascular benefit threshold of 1g EPA+DHA, you need 3–4 such capsules. Triple-strength products (550mg EPA+330mg DHA/capsule) reach the threshold in 1–2 capsules.

Are ALA-based plant omega-3 supplements effective?

ALA (from flaxseed, chia) converts to EPA at only 5–10% efficiency in most adults, and to DHA at <1%. Plant-based 'omega-3' products are not equivalent to fish oil for cardiovascular or cognitive endpoints. Algal omega-3 (DHA-rich) is the only plant-based option with direct activity.

How do I know if my fish oil is rancid?

Rancid fish oil has a TOTOX score above 26. Visual signs include a strong fishy smell and dark colour. Buy IFOS-certified products with published batch TOTOX data. Store in the refrigerator after opening and check the expiry date.
