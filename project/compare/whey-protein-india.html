Frequently asked questions

What is the best whey protein in India in 2026?

Nakpro Gold Whey (score 7.9) for value — Eurofins-verified, no amino spiking. Dymatize ISO100 (score 8.3) for isolate purity — NSF certified, hydrolyzed. AS-IT-IS Whey 80% (score 8.1) for honest concentrate at the lowest cost.

Is whey isolate better than concentrate for Indian consumers?

For lactose-sensitive individuals (common in India), isolate is clinically preferable — it contains <1g lactose per serving vs 3–5g in concentrate. The ₹600–₹1,000/kg premium is justified for this group.

What is amino spiking and which Indian brands were caught?

Amino spiking adds cheap free amino acids (glycine, taurine, creatine) to inflate nitrogen readings on protein tests. Bigmuscles flagged in 2023 Trustified testing. MuscleBlaze Biozyme flagged in earlier testing. Both brands subsequently published clean COAs.

How much whey protein should I take per day?

The clinically supported range for muscle protein synthesis is 0.3–0.4g/kg bodyweight per meal, 3–4 times daily. Total protein need is 1.6–2.2g/kg/day; whey should supplement dietary protein, not replace it.
