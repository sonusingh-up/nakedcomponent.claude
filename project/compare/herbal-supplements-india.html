Frequently asked questions

Are herbal supplements regulated by FSSAI in India?

Yes. Herbal supplements sold in India must comply with FSSAI Food Safety and Standards (Health Supplements, Nutraceuticals, etc.) Regulations 2022. Products making disease claims require specific approval. FSSAI registration ≠ efficacy approval — it confirms manufacturing standards only.

Does Garcinia cambogia work for weight loss?

No. Multiple high-quality RCTs show no significant weight loss vs placebo at any dose. The FDA issued a safety warning in 2017. The proposed HCA mechanism (malonyl-CoA inhibition) does not translate to human fat loss in controlled feeding studies.

Is turmeric/curcumin worth supplementing for Indian consumers?

Curcumin has anti-inflammatory RCT data for osteoarthritis and metabolic syndrome. However, bioavailability of standard curcumin is very low (<1%). Products using piperine (BioPerine) or phospholipid complexes (Meriva) show meaningfully higher absorption. Standardisation to 95% curcuminoids is the minimum required specification.

What is the evidence for Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri) in India?

Brahmi has positive RCT data for cognitive function — specifically working memory and processing speed — at 300–450mg standardised extract daily over 12 weeks. Results are most consistent in older adults. Look for products standardised to ≥20% bacosides.
