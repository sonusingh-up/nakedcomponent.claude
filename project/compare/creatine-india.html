Frequently asked questions

What is the best creatine supplement in India in 2026?

GNC Pro Performance Creatine (score 8.7) — Creapure-certified, correctly dosed at 5g, consistent across 3 tested batches. AS-IT-IS is the best value option at ₹9.2/dose with a NABL COA.

Is creatine HCl better than creatine monohydrate?

No RCT demonstrates creatine HCl superiority over monohydrate at equivalent doses. HCl products cost 2–4x more per effective dose. The published evidence base for monohydrate exceeds 200 RCTs.

What is a clinically effective daily dose of creatine?

3–5g daily for maintenance after an optional loading phase. The ISSN position stand (2017) supports 3g/day as the minimum effective dose for phosphocreatine saturation over 28 days.

Should I buy Creapure creatine or a generic Indian brand?

Creapure guarantees ≥99.9% purity via AlzChem's licensed supply chain. Indian brands like AS-IT-IS with NABL-accredited COAs achieve equivalent purity at significantly lower cost. Both are valid choices.
