Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Vitamin D2 and Vitamin D3?

D3 (cholecalciferol) raises serum 25(OH)D levels approximately twice as effectively as D2 (ergocalciferol) and has a longer half-life. ICMR and NICE guidelines recommend D3 for supplementation. Products using D2 are suboptimal regardless of dose.

Why is methylcobalamin better than cyanocobalamin for Indian vegetarians?

Methylcobalamin is the active, neurologically-relevant form of B12. Cyanocobalamin requires hepatic conversion. For vegetarians with borderline deficiency — the majority of Indian B12-deficient adults — methylcobalamin at adequate dose (500mcg+) is the preferred form.

Which multivitamin is best for Indian adults in 2026?

Scores above 7.5 go to products using D3, methylcobalamin B12, magnesium glycinate or citrate, and correct iron levels. HK Vitals (score 6.8) passes basic benchmarks but uses D2 and cyanocobalamin. TrueBasics and Carbamide Forte score higher on form quality.

How much Vitamin D do Indian adults actually need?

The ICMR 2020 RDA is 600 IU/day; the 2025 draft revision suggests 1500–2000 IU/day for adults with confirmed deficiency, which covers 70–90% of urban Indian adults. Weekly 60,000 IU sachets are pharmacologically legitimate but pharmacokinetically suboptimal vs daily dosing.
