Testing & certification

This is where Optimum Nutrition leads the India market. Much of the range carries Informed Choice certification, and select SKUs hold NSF Certified for Sport — the gold-standard banned-substance and quality programmes that very few brands sold in India can claim. Gold Standard Whey has also drawn high marks in independent Indian assays (Unbox Health rated it A+). On pure independent verification, nothing domestic matches it.

The caveat here isn't the testing — it's authenticity. ON is one of the most counterfeited supplement brands in India, and a fake unit voids every certification above. Buy only through the authorised ON India channel or its official Flipkart/Amazon storefronts, treat heavily discounted "deals" with suspicion, and confirm the certification applies to the exact SKU and batch in your hands.

Frequently asked questions
Is Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard Whey worth the premium price?
The quality and certification are genuine — Gold Standard Whey is consistently label-accurate and carries Informed Choice testing. But you pay a clear premium per gram. If certification and a globally proven product matter most, it's worth it; if price per gram is your priority, domestic verified whey such as AS-IT-IS is materially cheaper.
How do I avoid fake Optimum Nutrition in India?
ON is one of the most counterfeited supplement brands in India. Buy only from the authorised ON India store or its official Flipkart/Amazon storefronts, check the importer and batch details, and be sceptical of prices well below market. Grey-market units carry a real adulteration risk.
Is Gold Standard Whey a true isolate?
No. Gold Standard 100% Whey is a blend with whey protein isolate as the primary source, plus concentrate and whey peptides. If you want a pure isolate, choose Gold Standard 100% Isolate. For most goals the blend is perfectly adequate — check the total protein per scoop.
Is Optimum Nutrition third-party tested for the India market?
Much of the range carries Informed Choice certification, with NSF Certified for Sport on select SKUs — the strongest independent stack among brands sold in India. Certification scope varies by product, so confirm it for the specific SKU and that you're buying an authentic, authorised unit.
Optimum Nutrition vs AS-IT-IS or MuscleBlaze — which should I buy?
ON has the strongest certification and a globally proven product, but at a premium. AS-IT-IS offers the best price per gram with per-batch COAs, and MuscleBlaze the widest availability. Choose ON for certification and pay the premium; choose AS-IT-IS or Nakpro for verified value.
Opti-Men or Opti-Women — which multivitamin?
They're gender-formulated: Opti-Men carries higher doses of some nutrients aimed at men, Opti-Women is tuned with iron and nutrients aimed at women. Both are competently formulated; pick the one matched to your profile rather than assuming one is "stronger".
