Testing & certification

OZiva's challenge isn't legality — it's verifiability. It's FSSAI-licensed and slickly produced, but its formulas lean heavily on proprietary blends that disclose only a combined weight, so you cannot confirm the dose of any single active. The marketing leans on terms like 'bioavailable' and 'clean label' that imply clinical-grade rigour.
