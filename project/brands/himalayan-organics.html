The bottom line

Himalayan Organics is flagged primarily for misleading positioning. Its plant-based 'omega-3' is ALA-only — marketed with language that implies equivalence to EPA+DHA fish oil, which it is not, since ALA→EPA conversion in humans is below ~10%. Its 'organic' labelling isn't consistently backed by accredited certification, its herbal extracts are generic and unstandardised, and it publishes no independent COA. The products aren't necessarily harmful, but the claims overstate what they deliver — buy on accurate information, which the brand doesn't provide.
