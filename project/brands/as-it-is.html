Testing & certification

This is where AS-IT-IS separates itself from most of the Indian market. Rather than relying on a single brand-commissioned report, it publishes per-batch certificates of analysis tested at NABL-accredited Eurofins India — the same lab infrastructure used by the more credible imported brands — and carries an independent Trustified listing on top of that.

The raw, unflavoured positioning is a testing advantage in itself: with no flavour systems, sweeteners, or proprietary "matrix" blends, there is simply less to obscure. Frequently asked questions
Is AS-IT-IS Nutrition a legit and safe brand?
Yes. AS-IT-IS is FSSAI licensed and publishes batch certificates of analysis tested at NABL-accredited Eurofins India. It's also listed by independent verifier Trustified. On our rubric it scores 8.6/10 with no flags — among the most transparent supplement brands sold in India.
Is AS-IT-IS whey an isolate or a concentrate?
The flagship is Whey Protein Concentrate 80% (WPC80) — a concentrate at roughly 80% protein by weight, not an isolate. If you specifically need a 90%+ isolate, choose a dedicated WPI. For most goals, a well-tested WPC80 is the better value.
Why is AS-IT-IS cheaper than most whey and creatine brands?
It sells raw, unflavoured ingredients with minimal additives and very little marketing spend. You're paying for protein and creatine rather than flavouring systems, proprietary blends, and influencer campaigns — which is why its price per gram of active is among the lowest in India.
Where can I buy authentic AS-IT-IS products?
Buy from the official AS-IT-IS storefront on Amazon.in or the brand's own website, and confirm a current batch COA is available for the product. Avoid unfamiliar third-party sellers to reduce the risk of expired or counterfeit stock.
Does AS-IT-IS test for heavy metals and banned substances?
AS-IT-IS publishes batch COAs from NABL-accredited Eurofins India and is listed by Trustified. Testing scope varies by product and batch, so check the specific COA for the SKU you're buying to confirm exactly what was tested.
Is unflavoured whey protein worth it?
It's a trade-off. Unflavoured whey mixes less pleasantly on its own but contains no sweeteners, flavours, or fillers, costs less per gram of protein, and lets you control taste by blending it into milk, coffee, or smoothies. If taste is your priority, a flavoured product may suit you better.
