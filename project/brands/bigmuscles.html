Frequently asked questions
Why is Bigmuscles flagged if its tests pass?
Because the tests that pass are brand-commissioned — the brand chooses the sample and timing — while an independently-initiated blind-purchase test failed on protein content in 2023. A blind purchase off the shelf is a stronger signal than a brand-selected sample, and that discrepancy is unresolved.
Is Bigmuscles whey safe?
It carries Informed Choice for banned substances, so the concern isn't contamination — it's whether the protein content matches the label. That's what the 2023 independent fail called into question and what we want re-tested independently.
What's the difference between brand-commissioned and independent testing?
Brand-commissioned testing lets the brand pick which sample to send and when. Independently-initiated blind-purchase testing buys the product off the shelf anonymously — much harder to game, and the gold standard for verifying protein content.
What should I buy instead?
A brand with independently-verified protein: AS-IT-IS (per-batch COAs), Nakpro (Trustified/Eurofins), or MuscleBlaze's Trustified-certified SKUs.
