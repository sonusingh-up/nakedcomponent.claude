The bottom line

GNC trades on a legacy name, but the current India proposition doesn't justify the premium. You're paying for the brand, not for testing or value.

Frequently asked questions
Is GNC worth the price in India?
For most products, no — the brand premium isn't justified by India-market testing, and items like creatine cost roughly 3× the domestic rate. The exception is a few competent staples like its Triple Strength Omega-3.
Is GNC third-party tested for India?
Some US-market GNC products carry NSF or BSCG certification, but the India-market SKUs aren't independently certified for the domestic market. Don't assume the US certification applies here.
What's the best GNC product?
Its Triple Strength Omega-3 is the standout — correctly dosed with high EPA/DHA. Even then, compare the per-softgel EPA/DHA against cheaper rivals before paying the premium.
GNC or NOW Foods?
NOW Foods, for value and breadth — it's GMP-tested and delivers the same nutrients at a fraction of GNC's price. GNC only makes sense if you specifically want one of its staples from a physical store.

Our verdict

GNC is coasting on a legacy name. A couple of products — notably its Triple Strength Omega-3 — are genuinely good, but the range is mostly generic at premium prices, dotted with proprietary blends, and uncertified for India. Buy the specific staples if you must; for value and verification, NOW or a domestic brand wins comfortably.
