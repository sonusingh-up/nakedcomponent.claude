NOW Foods runs one of the more credible quality operations among imported brands: its facilities are NSF GMP-registered, it operates extensive in-house laboratories, and it backs that with external verification. Certificates of analysis are available on request rather than published per batch, but the testing infrastructure behind them is real and long-established.

Frequently asked questions
Is NOW Foods Vitamin D3 5,000 IU too high a dose?
For India's high deficiency burden, 5,000 IU is a common corrective dose, but it's best matched to a blood test. Many people maintain on less; check your 25-OH-D level and pair long-term D3 with vitamin K2.
Is NOW Foods third-party tested?
NOW's facilities are NSF GMP-registered with extensive in-house and external testing, and COAs are available on request. It's a credible standard, though not published per-batch like some Indian brands.
How do I avoid fake or expired NOW Foods in India?
Buy from the authorised NOW India distributor or a reputable importer's official storefront, check the import sticker and expiry date, and avoid unusually cheap third-party listings.
Is NOW Foods fish oil real EPA+DHA or just 'fish oil'?
It provides actual EPA and DHA (roughly 180/120 per softgel), molecularly distilled — not ALA. Compare the EPA/DHA split, not the headline mg, when judging any fish oil.
NOW Foods or a domestic brand for vitamins?
NOW offers the broadest verified range and standout value on D3 and fish oil. For single commodity vitamins a domestic brand is usually cheaper; for a wide, correctly-formulated range, NOW is hard to beat in the import segment.
