Testing & certification

Nakpro's headline strength is that its protein is checked by someone other than Nakpro. Through Trustified-coordinated Eurofins India testing, both the protein quantity and the full amino acid profile are independently assayed — the distinction that separates genuine verification from a brand-commissioned certificate. That is rare among Indian domestic protein brands and is the single biggest reason it earns a place in our recommended tier.

FSSAI licensed Eurofins / NABL tested Trustified listed Protein + amino verified
Eurofins India (NABL-accredited) via Trustified. Protein quantity + amino acid profile verified. FSSAI licensed with food-grade manufacturing and transparent ingredient sourcing.

Two honest caveats. First, the testing rigour is strongest on the whey line — the vitamins, fish oil and performance SKUs are not held to the same independent standard, so treat those labels at face value. Second, Nakpro publishes fewer per-batch COAs than AS-IT-IS's batch-by-batch model; the verification is real, but check the latest report for the exact product you're buying rather than assuming every batch is covered.

Frequently asked questions
Is Nakpro whey independently third-party tested?
Yes. Nakpro's whey is tested through Trustified-coordinated Eurofins India assays that verify both protein quantity and the amino acid profile. That's independent verification, not just a brand-commissioned report — which is why it scores 9/10 on our third-party testing dimension.
Is Nakpro Gold an isolate or a concentrate?
Despite the premium "Gold" name, it's a whey protein concentrate blend, not an isolate. If you specifically need a 90%+ isolate, choose Nakpro Platinum Whey Isolate instead. For most goals a verified concentrate is the better value.
Is Nakpro good value compared with AS-IT-IS and MuscleBlaze?
Nakpro offers strong value for independently verified domestic whey. It's slightly behind AS-IT-IS on pure price per gram of raw, unflavoured protein, but ahead of many competitors on independent testing. It scores 8.2/10 overall, just behind AS-IT-IS at 8.6.
Where can I buy authentic Nakpro products?
Buy from Nakpro's official Amazon.in storefront or its own website, and check the latest Trustified/Eurofins report for the specific product. Avoid unfamiliar third-party sellers to reduce the risk of expired or counterfeit stock.
Does Nakpro test for heavy metals and banned substances?
Nakpro's Trustified/Eurofins testing focuses on protein quantity and amino acid profile. Contaminant panels such as heavy metals vary by product and batch, so check the specific report for the SKU you're buying to confirm exactly what was tested.
Is flavoured Nakpro whey worth it over raw whey?
It's a trade-off. Flavoured whey mixes and tastes better but costs more per gram of protein. Nakpro's raw, unflavoured concentrate is the cheapest per gram in its range; the flavoured "Gold" and Impact lines cost more for the taste and convenience.

Our verdict

If you want independently verified protein from an Indian brand and don't mind paying a little more than raw, unflavoured tubs, Nakpro is a strong, honest pick. Just buy the Platinum line if you specifically need an isolate — and don't let the word "Gold" make the decision for you.
