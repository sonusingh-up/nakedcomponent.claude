# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Indian adults, health-conscious fitness enthusiasts, and biohackers looking for independent, evidence-based supplement advice. They want to cut through marketing hype, understand raw material origins (e.g., Creapure vs. generic), and calculate cost-per-effective-dose under Indian pricing and climate conditions.

## Product Purpose
Naked Compound exists to provide unbiased, peer-reviewed analysis of supplements in India. It compiles third-party lab test certificates, clinical trial evidence, and precise formulations to calculate an objective score for every product, making trust in advertising unnecessary.

## Brand Personality
* **Meticulous** (Scientific precision, precise numbers, depth of citation)
* **Evidence-driven** (Focus on human clinical trials, RCTs, and statistical evidence)
* **Transparent** (Public methodology, open scoring formulas, no hidden commercial bias)

## Anti-references
* Comically loud SaaS marketing pages with giant 96px display headings
* Over-rounded (32px+) card designs and floating glassmorphism shapes
* AI-scaffolded layouts containing tracked all-caps kicker eyebrows above every section
* Generic blue/orange pharmaceutical designs or overly saturated supplement store aesthetics

## Design Principles
1. **Clinical Restraint**: Let the data lead. Rely on strict grid layouts, clean borders, and high typographic contrast. No unnecessary decorations.
2. **Context-Rich Details**: Highlight evidence tiers (RCT, Meta-analysis, Observational) and precise numbers (e.g. ₹/dose, elemental content) using clear badges and tooltips.
3. **Muted Technical Aesthetic**: Combine clear editorial serif titles with JetBrains Mono tags and chips to evoke a research journal or laboratory logbook feeling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
* Target WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance.
* Maintain text contrast ratios of at least 4.5:1 for body copy and 3:1 for large text.
* Ensure all interactive hover animations and transitions respect `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
